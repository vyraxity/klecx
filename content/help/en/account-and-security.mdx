# Account & Security

## Signing In and Passwords

- You can sign in with an email address and password, or with one of the other accounts offered on the sign-in page.
- Passwords must be at least 10 characters long. A longer passphrase is the safest choice.
- To change your password, open **Settings → Security**, enter your current password and the new one. Changing it signs you out on every other device, and we email you to say it changed.
- To change a forgotten password, choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the link in the email. Resetting your password signs you out on every other device.
- If you only sign in with another account, you can add a password in **Settings → Security**: choose it, then enter the 6-digit code we email you (valid 10 minutes, 5 tries). After that you can also sign in with your email address and that password.
- **Settings → Security → Sign-in methods** connects or disconnects the other accounts you sign in with. You always keep at least one way to sign in.
- Password reset emails are limited to 3 requests per hour.

To change your email address, open **Settings → Profile** and choose **Change email**. We send a link to your current address to approve the change, then a link to the new address to confirm it: your email changes only after both. Your username can be chosen once and then stays the same.

## Two-factor Sign-in

Two-factor sign-in adds a second step after your password: a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. Someone who learns your password still can't open your account without your phone.

- **Turn it on** in **Settings → Security → Two-factor sign-in**: enter your password, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (any app that supports time-based codes), and enter the code it shows.
- **Save your backup codes.** You get 10 one-time codes when you turn it on. If you lose your phone, each code lets you sign in once. You can make new ones (the old ones stop working) in the same place.
- **Trusted devices:** when you sign in, you can tick **Don't ask again on this device for 30 days**.
- **Turn it off** in the same place, with your password. We email you whenever two-factor sign-in is turned on or off, or new backup codes are made.
- Two-factor sign-in protects signing in with your email and password, so it needs a password on the account. Signing in with a connected account (the other sign-in methods on the sign-in page) uses that account's own protection: turn on two-step verification there too.
- **Lost your phone and your backup codes?** Write to %%SUPPORT_EMAIL%% from your account's email address. We check that it's you before removing two-factor sign-in, and we email you when it's removed.

## Sessions & Devices

**Settings → Sessions & devices** lists every device signed in to your account, with:

- The browser and operating system.
- A partly hidden IP address and the country, when known.
- When it was last active.

You can sign out a single device, or all other devices at once. Signing out all other devices asks for your password if you haven't signed in recently.

## Sign-in Alerts

When your account is used from a new browser, operating system or country, Klecx sends you a notification and an email. If it wasn't you, sign out that device in **Sessions & devices** and change your password. You can change which alerts you receive in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Deleting Your Data

- **Workspaces**: archive a workspace to hide it from your sidebar and lists without deleting anything; archiving a project hides its workspaces too. Archived workspaces and projects are listed in **Settings → Deleted & archived**, on the **Archived** tab, where **Unarchive** brings them back (the owner or an admin can do it). Or delete a workspace: deleting asks you to confirm; the workspace then stays on the **Deleted** tab of the same page for 30 days, where you can restore it, before it's erased for good. A deleted project works the same way (its workspaces aren't deleted).
- **A copy of your data**: in **Settings → Your data**, choose **Request a copy**. Klecx prepares a ZIP file with your profile, settings, workspaces (every answer, version, edit, comment and document), files, credit history and support requests, and emails you when it's ready. Download it within 24 hours; you can request one copy per day.
- **Your account**: in **Settings → Your data**, choose **Delete my account** and type your email address to confirm. You're signed out everywhere at once. For 30 days, the link we email you restores your account; after that, your workspaces, files, notes and settings are erased. A paid plan is cancelled at the end of the period you paid for. Workspaces or projects you share with other people must first be transferred (the other person accepts) or deleted.
- You can also write to %%PRIVACY_EMAIL%% from the email address of your account.

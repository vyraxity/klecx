# Billing & Cancellation

{{^billing}}

When paid plans launch, this article will explain how to subscribe, change plan, cancel and request a refund, and the full terms will be in our [Terms of Service](/terms).
{{/billing}}
{{#billing}}
## Subscribing

Open the [pricing page](/pricing) or **Settings → Plan & billing**, choose a plan and monthly or yearly billing. You pay on the secure page of our payment partner, Creem, which is the merchant of record. Prices are in USD, plus applicable taxes, which are calculated at checkout. Your plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed, and its credits are added at once.

Plans renew automatically every month or every year until you cancel. Credits renew every month, also on yearly plans, which get their credits month by month.

## Changing Plan

In **Settings → Plan & billing**, choose **Change plan**. Upgrades start right away: our payment partner charges the prorated difference for the rest of the current period, and the extra credits of the new plan are added to your balance at once.

To move to a smaller plan, or from yearly to monthly billing, cancel your plan and subscribe to the new one when the current period ends.

## Payment Methods and Invoices

**Settings → Plan & billing → Payment methods & invoices** opens our payment partner's portal, where you can update your card and download invoices. Cancelling in that portal ends your plan immediately: to keep it until the end of the period you paid for, cancel in Settings instead.

If a renewal payment fails, we tell you by email and in the app. Update your payment method within 3 days of the end of the period to keep your plan.

## Cancelling

In **Settings → Plan & billing**, choose **Cancel plan**. You keep your plan and its credits until the end of the period you paid for, and it won't renew. Until then, **Keep my plan** undoes the cancellation. Afterwards your account moves to the Free plan; your workspaces and content are kept.

## Refunds

- New subscribers get a full refund within 14 days of their first payment if they've used less than 20 % of its credits. Once per customer.
- A renewal is refunded within 7 days if you haven't used any credits since it renewed.
- Duplicate charges and billing errors are always refunded.

A full refund ends the paid plan immediately and removes the unused credits of that payment. To ask for one, choose **Request a refund** in **Settings → Plan & billing**, or write to %%BILLING_EMAIL%%. We reply within 3 business days; the money goes back to your original payment method, which usually takes your bank 5–10 business days. The full terms are in our [Terms of Service](/terms#subscriptions).
